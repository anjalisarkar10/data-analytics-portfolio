## Key Insights

- Month-to-month customers show highest churn
- Fiber optic customers churn more frequently
- Short-tenure customers are at highest risk
- Electronic check users churn more often

## Business Recommendations

- Encourage long-term contracts
- Improve onboarding for new customers
- Target high-risk customer segments
- Improve retention offers
